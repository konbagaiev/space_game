# Enemy shields (all enemy types) — carve 1/3 of the existing HP into a regenerating shield

**Feature id:** `2026-07-26-2301-enemy-shields`
**Branch:** `feature/2026-07-26-2301-enemy-shields`
**Status:** planned (this brief is the executable spec; the implementer needs no other context)

## Goal

Every enemy ship gets a shield, exactly like the player's Base shield: an absorbing buffer that eats
damage before the hull and, once **fully broken**, refills to full after a recharge time. The shield is
**carved out of the HP the enemy already has** — capacity = 1/3 of its catalog hull durability, hull =
the remaining 2/3 — so **a kill finished within 10 s of breaking the shield costs exactly the damage it
costs today** (a 30 HP basic pirate becomes 10 shield + 20 hull; a 150 HP mini boss 50 + 100; a 300 HP L4
heavy 100 + 200). The
player-visible effect: shots that land on a live shield produce a **cyan bubble ripple around the enemy**
and a **cyan hit flash** instead of the orange hull spark, and the floating enemy bar gains a **blue
shield strip above the red health strip** (purple + filling while the shield recharges). Nothing about
aim changes — the hitbox stays the hull. **This is not purely cosmetic:** a broken shield refills to full
10 s after the break (player-identical rule, and it keeps ticking under fire), so a *fast* kill costs
exactly the damage it costs today, while a fight dragging past that 10 s window costs up to another 1/3
of the enemy's max HP each time — see decision 2.

## Decisions (already settled — do not re-open)

1. **Split:** `shieldCap = Math.round(durability / 3)`, `hullMax = durability − shieldCap`. Integer, exact,
   total always equals today's durability. Flat rule for **every** enemy, bosses included.
2. **Recharge:** identical semantics to the player — only a **fully depleted** shield recharges; it banks
   time while broken and, at `rechargeSec`, refills to **full** and reactivates. A **partial** shield holds
   indefinitely. `ENEMY_SHIELD_RECHARGE_SEC = 10` for every enemy.
   **Read the rule precisely:** `shieldRecharge` (`components.js:74-79`) banks `dt` on *every* tick where
   `shieldValue <= 0`, and `applyShieldedDamage` only resets `_shieldRechargeAccum` **on the breaking hit**
   — damage that lands on the bare hull afterwards does **not** touch the timer. So the shield comes back
   **10 s after the break, even under continuous fire**. There is no "leave it alone" condition and no
   timer-reset-on-hit anywhere.
   **Accepted trade-off (maintainer ruling, shown the exact numbers and confirmed):** a fight that lasts
   longer than 10 s past a break is genuinely harder than today — the enemy regains its full shield
   capacity every 10 s: **+183 HP per 10 s for the second boss, +103 for the first boss, +100 for the
   advanced medium pirate, +50 for the mini boss, +10/+12 for the small pirates.** "Total effective HP is
   unchanged" is therefore only true of the **first** kill window (from full shield to death within 10 s of
   the break); beyond that, long fights cost more damage. This is intended ("shields come back"), the
   **player's** shield semantics are deliberately left untouched (the asymmetry is accepted), and both
   facts must be stated plainly in SUMMARY, DECISIONS and the CHANGELOG — never in "sustained burst" /
   "if you disengage" framing.
3. **Damage routing is lossless.** Damage absorbs into the shield first and **spills the excess to the
   hull in the same tick** — no rounding, no clamping, no per-hit cap, and the death test stays `hp <= 0`.
   This is the invariant that keeps time-to-kill (and therefore the recorded Level-0 intro) identical.
4. **Hitbox unchanged.** Enemies keep the hull OBB swept test. We do **not** adopt the player's
   `SHIELD_RADIUS = 4` sphere interception — widening every enemy's hitbox would change aim feel. The
   drawn enemy bubble is therefore sized **snug to the hull** (≈ `broadR`) so the "shot stopped at the
   surface" mismatch stays small.
5. **Bubble visuals:** enemies get **no idle Fresnel rim** (the always-on rim stays a player-exclusive
   read). An enemy bubble is invisible until a hit is absorbed, then plays the ~1 s ripple/flash and
   disappears. Concurrency cap by graphics tier: **High 6 / Balance 3 / Performance 0**, oldest slot
   recycled. An enemy that can't get a slot still gets the HP-bar shield strip and the cyan hit flash.
6. **Sound: deferred, out of scope.** Do **not** add a synth voice, an audio asset, a `sounds`/`sound_map`
   row, or touch the player's absorb sound. Add a ROADMAP entry instead (see Step 9). The distinct
   **visual** flash for absorbed-vs-hull hits IS in scope.
7. **No DB / catalog / migration change.** The shield is derived client-side at spawn from the hull's
   `durability`. Enemy loot drops, resale prices and the `Pirate hull` item are untouched.
8. **Rockets do not get a shield.** Enemy rockets keep their own `r.hp` (shot down at 0) — they are
   projectiles, not ships. `projectiles.js` rocket-vs-bullet interception at `sim.js:551` is untouched.
9. **Warping enemies stay fully invulnerable** (the `e.warping` guards are unchanged); they spawn with a
   full shield and simply can't be hit.

```js
// Enemy shields are DERIVED, not catalog data: a ship's hull durability is SPLIT into a shield buffer
// (1/3, rounded) + the remaining hull, so killing it inside one shield cycle costs exactly the damage it
// cost before shields (a refill after a break adds another shield's worth — see DECISIONS §74).
// Integer + exact: shieldCap + hullMax === durability for every value.
export const ENEMY_SHIELD_FRACTION = 1 / 3;
export const ENEMY_SHIELD_RECHARGE_SEC = 10; // a BROKEN enemy shield refills to full after this long

export function enemyShieldSplit(durability) {
  if (!(durability > 0)) return { shieldCap: 0, hullMax: durability || 0 };
  const shieldCap = Math.round(durability * ENEMY_SHIELD_FRACTION);
  return { shieldCap, hullMax: durability - shieldCap };
}
```

### Step 5 — Enemy shield bubbles (`client/src/shield-fx.js`)

Extend the existing module rather than adding a new one — it already owns the shader.

0. **FIRST, fix the module clock — the enemy path is unusable without this.** Today
   `updateShieldBubble(dtSec)` (`shield-fx.js:111-113`) reads:

```js
export function updateShieldBubble(dtSec) {
  if (!bubble) return;   // <-- bails BEFORE the clock advances
  time += dtSec;
```
   and `bubble` is only created by `ensureBubble()`, which is reached solely from `registerShieldImpact` /
   `spawnShieldReady` — i.e. **only after the PLAYER has taken an absorbed hit.** Since the player almost
   always shoots before being shot, `time` is still `0` during the first exchange of every run. An enemy
   ripple registered then would get `impactStart = 0` and `slot.until = 1.0` with `uTime` frozen at `0`, so
   the shader's `age` never grows and `time >= slot.until` is never true — **the bubble would stick to the
   enemy, fully bright, forever**, until the player is first hit. Move the clock **above** the guard:

```js
export function updateShieldBubble(dtSec) {
  time += dtSec;         // module clock: ALWAYS advances (the enemy bubbles share it; see updateEnemyShieldBubbles)
  if (!bubble) return;   // no player bubble built yet (player never hit) — nothing else to do
```
   This is behaviour-neutral for the player path (every read of `time` there is relative to another
   `time` sample) and it makes the clock exist from frame 1.

1. **Extract the material factory.** Move the `uniforms` object construction out of `ensureBubble()` into
   `function makeBubbleMaterial(impactDir, impactStart, impactBroke)` returning a `THREE.ShaderMaterial`
   with the **same `vert`/`frag` sources** (unchanged text ⇒ three.js reuses one compiled program for the
   player and all enemy bubbles). The player path calls it with its existing module-level arrays.

   `main.js` passes `0`, so an in-flight ripple freezes instead of expiring — matching the player bubble.

   Plus edge cases: `enemyShieldSplit(0)` and `enemyShieldSplit(undefined)` → `{ shieldCap: 0, hullMax: 0 }`.
2. **Damage-to-kill is unchanged** (the load-bearing test). Helper:

```js
const makeEnemy = (d) => { const { shieldCap, hullMax } = enemyShieldSplit(d);
  return { shield: shieldCap ? { capacity: shieldCap, rechargeSec: 10 } : null,
           _shieldValue: shieldCap, _shieldRechargeAccum: 0, hp: hullMax, maxHp: hullMax }; };
const killWith = (d, perHit) => { const e = makeEnemy(d); let hits = 0;
  while (e.hp > 0) { applyShieldedDamage(e, perHit); hits++; if (hits > 10000) throw new Error('runaway'); }
  return { hits, dealt: hits * perHit, overkill: -e.hp }; };
```
   For every durability in `[30, 36, 150, 300, 310, 550]` × every per-hit power in `[6, 7, 13, 40, 100]`:
   `hits === Math.ceil(d / perHit)` (identical to the pre-shield `hitsToKill(d, perHit)` — assert against
   `hitsToKill` from the same module) **and** `dealt - overkill === d` (every point of damage is
   accounted for: nothing swallowed, nothing conjured).
3. **Boundary cases:** a single **overkill** hit (`perHit = d + 50`) kills in 1 hit; an **exact-break** hit
   (`perHit === shieldCap`) leaves `hp === hullMax` untouched, `_shieldValue === 0`, `broke === true`,
   `_shieldRechargeAccum === 0`; the next hit goes 100% to the hull. **Multi-hit weapon** (triple spiral:
   3×40 in one tick) on a 150 HP mini boss: after three `applyShieldedDamage(e, 40)` the enemy has taken
   exactly 120 of its 150.
4. **Partial shields never recharge; broken ones refill:** `shieldRecharge(shieldCap - 1, shieldCap, 10, 5, 0)`
   → no change, `accum 0`; `shieldRecharge(0, shieldCap, 10, 10, 0)` → `{ shieldValue: shieldCap, accum: 0 }`.
5. **Mass is untouched by the derived shield:** build a ship object with `hull/engine/thruster` weights and
   a `shield: { capacity: 10, rechargeSec: 10 }` (no `weight`), assert `shipMass()` equals the same ship
   with `shield: null`, and that `deriveDrive()` yields identical `acceleration`/`turnRate`. **This is the
   guard that the intro replay can't drift through changed enemy speed.**
6. Keep/rename the existing `applyPlayerDamage` tests to `applyShieldedDamage` (behaviour identical).

### Step 10 — Replay/intro impact (mandatory analysis, keep this section in the plan)

This change touches the **sim damage path**, so it is exactly the class of change that has desynced the
recorded Level-0 intro before (DECISIONS §73). The intro is an input replay: the same seed + the same
input keys re-run through the real `sim.update()`; anything that changes *when* an enemy dies, *when* a
bullet exists, or *what the seeded RNG stream is consumed by* breaks it.

Why the *damage math* is tick-identical:
- **Total HP per enemy is unchanged** (Step 1 test 1) and **damage routing is lossless with same-tick
  spill** (test 2), so the Nth bullet that killed an enemy before still kills it now — same tick.
- **Death threshold unchanged** (`enemies[i].hp <= 0`, `sim.js:725`).
- **Mass/handling unchanged** (weightless derived shield; test 5) → identical enemy movement.
- **No new RNG draws.** `applyShieldedDamage`, `shieldRecharge`, `enemyShieldSplit`, the bubble pool, the
  HP-bar strip and `spawnExplosion` (with a colour argument) all contain zero `Math.random()`/`simRandom()`
  calls. The bubble is advanced from the **native frame delta** in `main.js`, never from sim `dt`, and it
  never writes sim state — the same contract as the player bubble (DECISIONS §68/§73).
- **`e.maxHp` semantics change (now hull-only) affects render only** — grep confirms the sole gameplay-side
  reader of enemy `maxHp` is `hud.js`; `repairTick` is player-only.

**The one REAL divergence risk — a legitimate shield refill (do not hand-wave it).** The recharge timer
runs from the breaking hit regardless of incoming fire, so any enemy in the trace that stays alive **≥10 s
after its shield breaks** gets its shield back and needs extra hits. This is *plausible* here, not
theoretical: the canonical trace re-sims ~50 s for 4 kills, the starter `Basic kinetic` deals exactly
**10** damage (`catalog_seed.js` weapon id 1) and a basic pirate's shield is exactly **10**, so the very
first bullet that connects breaks that enemy's shield for **0 hull damage** and starts its 10 s clock. If
the recorded input then spends ≥10 s on another target before finishing that one, the shield returns and
the trace runs out of bullets → `playDone` with enemies alive. **Correct, lossless math can produce this
failure.** The plan does not claim it cannot happen; it gives a way to tell the two causes apart.

**Triage procedure if `22-intro-replay` fails (in order, do not skip):**
1. **Run the unit tests first** (`cd client && node --test`). If the split/damage-to-kill/mass tests are
   green, the damage math is provably lossless and mass is unchanged — the failure is **not** rounding or
   a lost damage point.
2. **Read the refill counter.** In the failing scenario (or a quick `?playback` run), read
   `window.__game.enemyShieldRefills` (Step 4 / Step 9b) at the end of the re-sim, and add it to
   `25-enemy-shield`'s neighbours by printing it in the failure output:
   - **`enemyShieldRefills === 0`** → no shield ever came back, so the divergence is something else
     (a lost/extra RNG draw, changed mass, a changed death tick). **This is a real bug in the change —
     find it.** Suspects, in order: an accidental `Math.random()`/`simRandom()` in the new code paths, a
     `weight` sneaking onto the derived shield object, an altered `hp <= 0` test, or an FX call moved
     inside the sim tick.
   - **`enemyShieldRefills > 0`** → the intro genuinely diverged because an enemy's shield came back.
     The code is behaving as designed. **STOP and escalate to the maintainer** with the counter value and
     which enemy refilled. Two options, maintainer's call only:
     (a) **re-record the intro trace** (DECISIONS §73 + the `record-playback` skill) — this invalidates the
     old trace and needs explicit sign-off plus a re-run of the guard and a live intro playthrough; or
     (b) **re-tune `ENEMY_SHIELD_RECHARGE_SEC`** (the single knob) so the intro stays inside the window.
   - **Never re-record unilaterally**, and never "fix" the guard by loosening its assertions.

Also run the intro **live** once after deploy (Stage 9 of the pipeline): reset progress → play the intro
end-to-end → confirm it reaches victory and the Level 1 briefing.
